# Kenney City Kit (Commercial) — used in Cinder City

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial
- Version in use: 2.1 (downloaded 2026-10-16)
- License: CC0 (Creative Commons Zero) — see License.txt (copied from the
  in-pack License.txt). Free for commercial use, no attribution required.
- Files kept: the 21 GLB models actually used by Cinder City
  (14 `building-*`, 5 `building-skyscraper-*`, 2 `detail-parasol-*`) plus
  the shared `models/Textures/colormap.png` the GLBs reference.
  FBX variants, previews, and unused models were not kept.
- The GLBs are imported by Godot (see the adjacent .import files); the
  Cinder City builder scales one kit model to fit each building's exact
  gameplay volume. Collision is unchanged (original voxel boxes).
